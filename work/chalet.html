Chalet Zolotaj Gorka

Full website UI/UX design from scratch

Chalet Zolotaj Gorka is a cozy vacation retreat offering private houses for rent, each featuring a hot tub and a peaceful natural setting. The client needed a modern, visually appealing website that would attract visitors, provide a smooth booking experience, and reflect the relaxing, luxurious vibe of their properties.

Live Site

Research & Discovery

Before jumping into design, I needed to understand:

  • The target audience (families, couples, groups of friends looking for nature + comfort)
  • The competitive landscape (how similar rental services present themselves online)
  • The client’s goals: drive more direct bookings, build brand trust, and convey a premium yet cozy experience.

Key Insights:

  • Photos and aesthetics are crucial in the booking decision.
  • Users want fast access to availability and pricing.
  • The vibe should feel natural, relaxing, and trustworthy.

UX Strategy & Wireframing

I mapped out the user journey from discovery to booking. Key UX considerations included:

  • Clear value proposition on the homepage
  • Easy-to-navigate structure (Home, About, Houses, Booking, Contact)
  • Quick access to essential info like location, amenities, and pricing

Then I created low-fidelity wireframes to:

  • Focus on content hierarchy
  • Test layout ideas
  • Align with the client on structure before diving into visuals

UI Design & Visual Language

This is where the magic happened.

I built a custom design system with:

  • Warm, earthy colors to evoke nature and calmness
  • Elegant typography to emphasize quality
  • Large, immersive photos of the houses and surroundings
  • Clean, consistent UI elements (buttons, cards, icons)

Every detail—from spacing to color gradients—was tailored to reflect the brand's unique atmosphere.

Responsive Design

The site was fully designed for:

  • Desktop
  • Tablet
  • Mobile

The mobile version wasn’t just a scaled-down version—it was optimized for touch, quick navigation, and on-the-go booking.

Results & Handoff

The final result was a beautiful, user-friendly website that:

  • Enhances trust with potential guests
  • Showcases the properties effectively
  • Supports smooth booking flow

All design files were neatly organized and handed off in Figma for easy implementation by developers.
