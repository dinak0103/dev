ADVGO42

Full website UI/UX design from scratch

ADVGO42 is a cutting-edge affiliate marketing platform aimed at helping clients grow their online revenue. I was responsible for crafting the entire user experience and interface of the website to reflect credibility, professionalism, and growth-focused messaging.

Live Site

Wireframes

Goal: Translate structure into visual layout without distractions.

  • Designed low-fidelity wireframes for key pages: Home, About, Services, Partners, and Contact.
  • Focused on content layout, CTA placement, and UX best practices

Visual Design & Branding

Goal: Reflect a modern, trustworthy, and professional brand.

  • Design System: Built reusable components (buttons, forms, colors, icons, typography).
  • Typography: Chose modern, readable fonts that convey trust.
  • Color Scheme: Developed a palette that reflects confidence and energy, primarily using shades of blue and orange.
  • Imagery & Icons: Used clean illustrations and iconography to support clarity.

High-Fidelity UI Design

  • Created pixel-perfect high-fidelity mockups.
  • Maintained visual hierarchy and consistency across the website.
  • Optimized layout for different screen sizes, focusing on responsiveness and mobile experience.

Mobile Design

  • Designed mobile versions for every page.
  • Prioritized readability, CTA accessibility, and scroll behavior for touch interfaces.
  • Ensured components adjusted well within smaller viewports while preserving brand identity.

Developer Handoff

  • Organized all assets and components clearly in Figma.
  • Worked closely with the development team to ensure the design was implemented accurately and responsively.
