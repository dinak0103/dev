Häggberg Kakel

Full website UI/UX design from scratch

Häggberg Kakel is a Swedish company specializing in tiling services. They needed a professional, modern, and trustworthy online presence to showcase their craftsmanship and attract local customers. I was responsible for creating the entire UI/UX for their new website from the ground up.

Live Site

Discovery & Research

To kick things off, I gathered insights about the business, target audience, and competitors.

  • Competitor Analysis: Reviewed 5 local and national competitors in the tiling/home renovation space
  • User Persona Creation: Defined ideal users—mostly homeowners aged 30–60 looking for reliable tiling services

Key Objectives:g

  • Establish trust and professionalism
  • Highlight completed projects visually
  • Make it easy to contact the company

Information Architecture

I defined the core structure of the website based on user goals and client needs:

Home, Projects, Services, About, Contact

I mapped out user flows to ensure easy access to key actions like viewing past work or requesting a quote.

Wireframes

Before diving into the visual design, I created high-fidelity wireframes to map out layout and content structure:

  • Focused on clarity and intuitive navigation
  • Emphasized a visual-first approach for the Gallery and Home pages

UI Design

Using Figma, I designed high-fidelity mockups for both desktop and mobile.

  • Visual Style: Clean, modern, Scandinavian-inspired
  • Color Palette: Neutral tones (white, grey) with warm accent colors for contrast
  • Typography: Clear, readable fonts with a professional tone
  • Imagery: High-quality project photos to establish credibility
  • Animations/Microinteractions: Subtle hover effects to guide user attention

Design Handoff

I organized all components, styles, and specs in Figma to ensure a smooth developer handoff:

  • Provided responsive versions for all screen sizes
  • Created a basic design system (buttons, typography, spacing, etc.)
